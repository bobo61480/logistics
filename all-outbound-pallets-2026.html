Counting Methodology

  • WH Trucking Request — "Pallet Type" column: "1×53'" trailer = 26 pallets, explicit "N PLTS/PALLETS" at face value, bare integer at face value, dimension cell (NxNxN) = 1 pallet. 490 rows, 490 pallets.
  • ULTA — No dedicated pallet column. Each row = 1 LTL shipment. Pallets estimated as ⌈Cartons ÷ 20⌉, minimum 1. 309 shipments → 725 estimated pallets.
  • IHERB — "QTY" column is the pallet count directly (confirmed large loads: 2, 4, 9–25 pallets). All 9 rows are July 2026.
  • B2B/E-COM Trucking — PLT column present; tab returned 0 data rows for 2026. Data may not yet be entered for this period. * Not included in total.
  • TRANSFERS — "PLT" column ("28 PLTS", "15 PLTS", etc.). Dates are MM/DD only — year resolved by sequential chronological order (sheet runs from late 2025 into 2026). Largest source: 992 pallets (weekly BP↔NJ warehouse transfers, typically 26–28 pallets each).
  • TJX/ROSS (Outbound Shipping Schedule) — "# of Pallets" column. Only "Trucking" rows counted (UPS/parcel rows excluded). Dates MM/DD treated as 2026.
  • Nationals (Order Progress) — No pallet column; each trucking-flagged row counted as 1 move. Low count reflects that many rows lack a confirmed pickup date and are therefore excluded. † Undercounts actual pallet volume.
  • TJX/ROSS Dimension — "PLT" column. August spike (137) reflects large multi-DC shipments.
  • Rows with CANCELLED status excluded in all tabs. Historical 2025 rows excluded.
